## Section A — Grammar & accuracy (20 marks)

**A1. Choose the correct word. (1 mark each)**
1. The team ( has / have ) been training every morning since Monday.
2. Neither of the boys ( was / were ) late for practice.
3. She is the girl ( who / which ) scored in the final minute.
4. He ran ( quick / quickly ) down the steps.
5. If I ( was / were ) taller, I would play centre.

**A2. Punctuate this passage. Add capitals, commas, full stops, question marks and speech marks. (8 marks)**
```
where are you going shouted mum from the kitchen
to the park said ah ming
in this rain she asked
its not raining he lied
```

**A3. Rewrite each sentence in the tense shown in brackets. (1 mark each)**
1. He eats three bowls of noodles. (past simple)
2. They play football. (future: going to)
3. She wrote the letter. (present perfect)
4. I am reading a comic. (past continuous)

## Section B — Vocabulary & expression (10 marks)

**B1. Replace the blue word with a more precise word. (1 mark each)**
1. The food at the night market was very **nice**.
2. He was **very happy** when he won the match.
3. The walk up Tai Mo Shan was **very tiring**.
4. The MTR was **very crowded** this morning.
5. The horror film was **very scary**.

**B2. Complete the simile or metaphor. (1 mark each)**
1. The traffic was as slow as ________________.
2. Her voice was a ________________ , cutting through the noise.
3. The dumplings were ________________ of the steamer. *(metaphor)*
4. The sun ________________ the harbour. *(strong verb, not "was")*

## Section C — Reading comprehension (20 marks)

Read the passage, then answer.

> **Text A**
>
> The old tram rattles up the hill as if it has all the time in the world. Tourists raise their phones; commuters don't look up. Inside, a boy of about twelve presses his forehead against the warm glass and watches the city slide away beneath him. Below, the harbour is a sheet of beaten metal. He has lived here all his life and he has never once grown tired of this climb. At the top, he will buy a custard tart, eat half of it, and save the rest for his grandmother, who cannot manage the hill any more. That, more than the view, is why he rides.

**C1.** Where is the boy? Use a phrase from the text. **(2)**
**C2.** What impression does the writer give of the tourists compared with the commuters? **(3)**
**C3.** The harbour is described as "a sheet of beaten metal". What does this suggest about how it looks? **(3)**
**C4.** Explain in your own words why the boy makes this journey. **(4)**
**C5.** *"as if it has all the time in the world"* — what does this tell us about the tram? **(3)**
**C6.** Find one detail that shows the boy goes up the hill often. Explain how it helps you know this. **(5)**

## Section D — Writing (free response, 20 marks)

**Choose ONE. Write 150–200 words.**

1. *Describe* your favourite place in Hong Kong, so that a reader who has never been there can picture it.
2. *Narrate* a story that ends with the words: "…and that was the last time I ever took a shortcut."

**Assessed on:** W1 (content/ideas), W2 (structure), W3 (vocabulary & sentences), W4 (fit to purpose), W5 (accuracy). See the band descriptors in `10-answer-key.md`.
