# 09 — Timed Mock Paper

*Week 8. Sit under exam conditions: no dictionary, no phone, timer visible. Two sittings recommended.*

---

## Sitting 1 — Paper 1 style: Reading (60 minutes, 40 marks)

### Text A

> Kwun Tong at seven in the morning smells of rain and bleach. The wet market traders hose down their stalls before the city wakes, and the water runs in dark ribbons between crates of cabbage and cages of pale, craning chickens. Mrs Ho has run the same stall for thirty-one years. She can tell you the price of everything and the name of almost everyone. When the estate across the road was demolished last spring, she stood in the empty stalls of her neighbours and cried — not for the buildings, she says, but for the habits. "You know a person by how they buy fish," she says. "Now I don't know anyone."

**Q1.** Where and when is this text set? **(2)**
**Q2.** Using the text, describe Mrs Ho. **(3)**
**Q3.** *"the water runs in dark ribbons"* — what does this image suggest? **(4)**
**Q4.** What impression does the writer give of how Mrs Ho feels about the demolition? Use evidence. **(5)**
**Q5.** *"Now I don't know anyone."* — explain the effect of this short sentence as the text's ending. **(6)**

### Text B (summary focus)

> The new community garden was built on an empty lot that had been used for parking. Within a season it changed the estate. Children who had played indoors now grew beans and argued over watering rotas. Elderly residents, many of whom had not spoken to a neighbour in years, found themselves with a reason to leave their flats. Rents on the surrounding blocks rose slightly as the area became more desirable. Some long-time tenants worried that this would price out the very people the garden had brought together.

**Q6.** Summarise the effects of the community garden on the estate. **(12 marks, limit 100 words.)**

### Q7. Extended response
*"Places matter more to us for the habits they hold than for the buildings they contain."* Using ideas from **both texts**, write about how far you agree. **(8 marks)**

## Sitting 2 — Paper 2 style: Writing (60 minutes, 40 marks)

**Section A — Directed writing (20 marks)**
Write a **letter to the editor** of a local newspaper arguing that more community spaces like the garden in Text B should be created in Hong Kong. Use ideas from the reading texts and your own experience. *(~250–300 words.)*
- Register: formal, persuasive
- Structure: letter conventions + 3 developed arguments + counter-argument answered

**Section B — Composition (20 marks)**
Choose **one**, 350–450 words:
1. *Describe* a place in Hong Kong that is disappearing.
2. *Describe* a market at the moment it closes for the day.
3. *Write a story that ends with the words:* "…and nobody ever mentioned it again."
4. *Write a story about a character who is given a second chance.*
